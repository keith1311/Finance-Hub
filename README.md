**Description:** A secure, full-stack personal finance and multi-wallet budgeting manager.
**Core Features:**
- **Multi-Wallet Ecosystem:** Manage separate cash flows, bank accounts, and digital wallets with inter-wallet transfers.
- **Automation:** Automated data entry and processing to minimize manual entry.
- **Advanced Analytics:** Real-time expense and income breakdown across daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly timeframes.
- **Smart Budgeting Engine:** Category calculations and threshold tracking to prevent overspending.
